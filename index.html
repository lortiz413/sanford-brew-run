    Beer & bites

    Your two main stops face each other on Sanford Avenue, right by the trolley stop. Tap lists rotate, so ask what is fresh and order a flight.

    Stop 1 ¬∑ 12:40 PMWednesday 11:30 AM to 10 PM

    Wop's Hops Brewing Company

    419 S Sanford Ave, Sanford, FL 32771

    Sanford's first microbrewery, open since 2014, with Italian pub food. The building was once a brothel and locals say a young boy haunts it, a good story for Halloween week. There is a giant dinosaur on the patio.

    Marco Luppolo IPATheir rotating single hop IPA series. Different hop each batch.
    Coconut beerThe one regulars keep mentioning. Ask if it is on.
    Hail CaesarAmerican blonde ale, 5.7%. Easy opener.
    NeroSchwarzbier (black lager), 5.2%. Dark but light bodied.

    To eat

    • Wopsicle: a meatball on a stick in tomato gravy. Their signature.
    • Pasta, pizza and subs if you want a real lunch.
    • Dessert with a kick: the Unholy Cannoli runs 13% ABV. Share it.

    Locals mention a Wednesday "one off" brew special. Ask the bartender if there is one tomorrow.

    Stop 2 ¬∑ 1:50 PMWednesday 11 AM to 10 PM

    Brewlando Sanford

    400 S Sanford Ave, Sanford, FL 32771

    The former Sanford Brewing Company, reopened by Brewlando in March 2025. Full restaurant, patio, and more than 20 beers brewed in house, plus small batch brews made on site.

    Wednesday is happy hour all day: 15% off all drinks. Good place to sit out the 2 to 3 PM storm window. Weekday happy hour is normally only 4 to 6 PM.
    Start with a flightPick four from the house list and ask for their newest small batch.
    MeadThe old Sanford Brewing tap list was known for meads. Ask if any remain.

    To snack

    • Fried pretzel with beer cheese dip
    • Beer Bang Shrimp
    • Beer Cheese Philly

    These were favorites when it was Sanford Brewing. Brewlando said it kept much of that menu, but confirm with your server.

    Wear

    • Light hooded rain jacket. Skip the umbrella: 35 mph gusts will flip it
    • Quick drying tee or short sleeve shirt underneath
    • Light chinos or quick dry shorts, not heavy denim that stays wet
    • Closed shoes with grip. Wet brick sidewalks downtown are slick
    • A snug cap under the hood if you want one; nothing loose

    Bring

    • Photo ID. Breweries check for 21 and over
    • Card or phone wallet for tickets and tabs
    • Charged phone plus a small battery pack
    • A zip bag for your phone and ticket in heavy rain
    • Water bottle for the train

    If thunder starts

    Stay inside the taproom until it passes. If you are at Sanford Station, wait under the platform canopy, not in the open parking lot. Trains run in rain; check live status if storms are heavy.

    If plans change

    Running late this morning

    Next train: 12:15 PM from Poinciana, arrives Sanford 1:35 PM, trolley at 1:45 PM, off at Sanford Ave & 5th at 2:08 PM. Go straight to Brewlando, skip Dees, and keep the same 4:08 PM trolley back.

    Missed the 4:08 trolley

    Catch it at the Information Center at 4:18 PM, or take an Uber or Lyft to Sanford Station (2720 W State Rd 46) for the 5:06 PM train. Arrives Poinciana 6:28 PM, still before sunset.

    Want more time in Sanford

    Stay until about 4:40 PM, then rideshare to the station for the 5:06 PM train. The 5:36 PM train reaches Poinciana at 6:58 PM, which is after sunset, so treat it as a last resort.

    Train delays

    Check live train status on sunrail.com or the SunRail app before heading to the platform. Bridge buses run when service is interrupted. If the train is running late, still go: trains run until 10:16 PM, but if the delay would push your arrival past 6:44 PM, consider a rideshare home instead.
